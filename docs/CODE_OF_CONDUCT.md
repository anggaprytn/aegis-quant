# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Aegis Quant is maintained as a technical project where people should be able to collaborate without harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks. Participate professionally, assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements, and keep feedback specific to the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes abusive or discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Concerns should be reported privately to the repository maintainers through the private contact channel provided by the repository host. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly as the maintainers can manage, with confidentiality respected where practical.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Retaliation against a good-faith report is not acceptable.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository spaces, issue and pull-request discussions, and project-related interactions where someone is representing Aegis Quant.
